I am Muzaffar Mirzaev, a software engineer working where digital twins, autonomous mobile robots (AMRs), AI vision and smart-factory systems meet. I build web and backend applications that connect operations teams, simulations, industrial devices and AI-enabled workflows.

My background combines full-stack product development with management information systems research: I hold a Ph.D. in Management Information Systems from Keimyung University and an MBA from the Asian Institute of Technology. Over the past several years I have shipped dashboards, real-time monitoring interfaces and 3D web applications for manufacturing, logistics and library systems, mostly with TypeScript, React, Next.js, NestJS, FastAPI and Python.

My current focus is AMR control systems, from order-to-mission dispatch and execution monitoring through recovery and traceability, and connecting that operational software to ROS 2 fleets, PLC equipment and FlexSim digital-twin simulation. I enjoy turning complex factory and robotics requirements into clear, reliable software.
