  • Learning to code in Ansan: weekends between study sessions Jun – Dec 2021 I took these photos between June and December 2021, while I was preparing to become a web developer. Most are from Ansan and a few are from Seoul, taken on weekends when I was not coding. The idea goes back to summer 2020. During my two-week quarantine in Korea I was inspired by Bekzod Sanakulov, who had come from a business background, started working as a full-stack developer in Busan, and is now the founder and CEO of DevEx academy. His guidance and mentorship stayed with me throughout. I trained mostly through self-study and YouTube videos, with individual online tutors from Tashkent and online IT training centers such as WebBrain in Tashkent and AbuTech in Jizzakh. Most days I spent the whole day in one of the nice Ediya cafes in Ansan with a caffè latte, learning and writing code. There were no vibe-coding tools back then; everything was done by hand. After about eight months of self-training, I started my first job on 10 January 2022 as a frontend developer at Delmetenz, an IT company in Asan. Ansan and Seoul, South Korea
