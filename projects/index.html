Robotics & Digital Twin

AMR Control System (ACS)

Principal Software Engineer · Robotics technology company, Seoul · Sep 2026 – present

A control system for autonomous mobile robot fleets. It turns factory orders into robot missions, dispatches and monitors them, supports recovery when something fails, and keeps a record of what happened.

Problem
An AMR fleet needs a layer between factory operations and the robots. Orders have to become missions, missions have to be dispatched and followed through execution, and operators need to see what is happening and recover when a robot, a station or a piece of equipment does not behave as planned. That layer should not be tied to a single robot vendor.
My contribution
Responsible for the architecture and development of the system across the operations dashboard, the backend services, and the integration layer toward robots, factory equipment and simulation.
Architecture
  • Operations application. A Next.js and TypeScript dashboard on a FastAPI backend with PostgreSQL, running as containerized services. It covers mission management, fleet operations, map and station management, and alarms and events.
  • Mission lifecycle. A mission is tracked from order and dispatch through execution to completion or failure, with recovery workflows for the failure cases and an audit trail for traceability.
  • Real-time state. Robot and mission status reach the dashboard over WebSocket.
  • Robot integration. A fleet-manager and robot-adapter layer separates the control logic from the robot side. Robots are integrated through ROS 2, with Nav2 navigation, AMCL localization and SLAM mapping.
  • Equipment integration. PLC and IoT devices are connected over industrial communication protocols to synchronize robot status, equipment signals, safety interlocks and operational events.
  • Simulation. Operational workflows are connected to a FlexSim digital-twin model.
Validation
The FlexSim integration is used to compare scenarios on KPIs such as throughput, utilization, waiting time and fleet sizing. The system is under active development, so no production results are reported here.

Customer, site and equipment details are left out for confidentiality.

Next.js · TypeScript · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · WebSocket · Docker · ROS 2 · Nav2 · AMCL · SLAM Toolbox · PLC / IoT · FlexSim
